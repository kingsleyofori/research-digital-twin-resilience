Digital Twin-Based Stochastic Energy Resilience Modelling of Cape Town Municipal Facilities

This interactive environment accompanies the study Digital Twin-Based Stochastic Energy Resilience Modelling of Cape Town Municipal Facilities under Load Shedding.

The digital twin represents municipal facilities and their modelled energy resilience characteristics using historical facility electricity-use data and historical electricity-disruption records.

Open Interactive Digital Twin
Note: The resilience classes shown on the map reflect differences in the facility-level resilience indicator and do not represent circuit-specific exposure to load shedding.
